# Soft Life Society: Real Reviews Kit

The fastest honest way to get your first 10 to 20 reviews. Fake reviews are illegal under the FTC rule on consumer reviews (in effect since October 2024, with penalties of over $50,000 per violation), and shoppers and platforms are getting better at spotting them. Real reviews also convert better, because they sound like your customers.

## 1. The Founding Reviewer program (get 10 to 20 reviews in 2 weeks)

Give your product free to women in your audience in exchange for an honest review. This is allowed as long as you disclose it.

**Who to invite:** email subscribers, followers who comment often, friends of friends who fit your customer (not close family), and past customers.

**Instagram / TikTok post or Story:**

> I'm looking for 15 Founding Reviewers for Soft Life Society. You'll get [product or bundle] free, and in return I ask for your honest review after 7 days. Good, bad, or in between, I want the truth. Comment "REVIEWER" or DM me to apply.

**DM or email to accepted reviewers:**

> Hi [name], thank you so much for being a Founding Reviewer. Here's your free access to [product]: [link]. Use it for about a week, then leave your honest review here: [review link]. The site will note that you received the product free, which keeps everything transparent. No pressure to be positive. Your real experience is what helps other women decide.

**Rules to follow**
- Don't require or reward only positive reviews.
- Every free-product review shows "Received this product free for an honest review."
- Don't have family, employees, or yourself write reviews.
- Don't delete negative reviews. Respond kindly instead. (You can remove spam, hate, or personal info.)

## 2. Post-purchase review emails

**Day 7 after purchase**

Subject: How's your soft life era going, [first name]?

> Hi [first name],
>
> It's been a week since you got [product]. I'd love to know how it's going.
>
> Would you take 60 seconds to leave an honest review? It helps other women find what they need, and it helps me make every product better.
>
> [Leave a review button]
>
> With love,
> D. Jones, Soft Life Society

**Day 14 follow-up (only if no review yet)**

Subject: One quick question

> Hi [first name], just one question: what's one thing [product] helped you with? Reply to this email or leave it here: [review link]. Thank you for being part of Soft Life Society.

## 3. Questions that get great reviews

Put these on the review form as optional prompts:
1. What was going on in your life before you got this?
2. What's one thing you did or changed because of it?
3. What was your favorite page or section?
4. Who would you recommend it to?

## 4. Using reviews on the site and on social media

- Show stars and reviews on product pages with a "Verified purchase" badge (the Claude Code prompt, section 10, builds this).
- Ask before sharing a review on social media, and use her first name and last initial only.
- Screenshots of real DMs are fine to share with permission. Blur her name and photo unless she says yes.
- Until you have reviews, use honest proof instead: "Created by D. Jones," "Every Box hand-packed by Dess," page previews, and "What's inside" lists.
